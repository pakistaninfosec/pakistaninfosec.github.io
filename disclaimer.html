AI-Generated Content

This site uses AI (currently Meta's LLaMA models via Groq) to automatically generate executive summaries, daily blog digests, and per-vulnerability technical analysis based on publicly available source data.

AI-generated summaries are a convenience layer, not a replacement for the original advisory. They may occasionally be incomplete, out of date, or contain inaccuracies inherent to automated text generation. Always cross-reference critical decisions against the original source (linked wherever possible) before acting.

Accuracy of Information

Threat and vulnerability data is aggregated automatically from third-party sources on a recurring schedule (approximately every 30 minutes). While we aim to keep this data current, we cannot guarantee that every entry reflects the most recent status of a given vulnerability, advisory, or exploit at every moment.

Severity ratings, "zero-day" flags, and "actively exploited" labels are derived from the source feeds (e.g., CISA KEV) and are only as accurate and current as those upstream sources.
